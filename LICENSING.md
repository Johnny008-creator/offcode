# Licensing status

The project owner has not selected an open-source license for Offcode's own
code. Public repository visibility does not itself grant an open-source
license. No additional license grant is made by this document.

Third-party components retain their own licenses. Their notices and license
texts are listed in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and
[docs/licenses/](docs/licenses/). Model weights, external services and optional
training dependencies are not bundled and have their own terms.
